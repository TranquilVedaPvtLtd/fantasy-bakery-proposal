The real problem is not demand. It is unstructured custom ordering.
- ⬥ -

Fantasy already has demand for custom cakes. Monthly revenue is around ₹3,00,000, with an average custom cake ticket size of roughly ₹2,000. That means customers are willing to pay for personal, occasion-led cakes. The issue is that most of this demand is still handled through WhatsApp.

Fantasy Cake Studio: a visual custom cake ordering journey.
- ⬥ -

Fantasy Cake Studio is the centre of the new proposal. It lets a customer co-create a cake instead of only describing it in a WhatsApp message. A parent can type “jungle theme cake for Aarav's 5th birthday”, upload a reference, choose size and flavour, and see design options before ordering.

Frequent questions.

How does the AI Cake Studio actually work?
The customer picks a theme (Jungle, Unicorn, Cocomelon, Anniversary, and so on). A curated library of 25 to 40 hand-approved AI cake designs renders instantly so they are never staring at a loading screen. If they want fresh designs, a "Generate new designs" button creates 6 more variations on demand in about 90 seconds. Once they pick a design, they get 3 customisation rounds (add the child's name in fondant, swap an element, change the colour). After the third round the design is locked, the price is calculated from your real ingredient costs, and the order moves to chef approval and payment. Complex designs still go through Fantasy feasibility review before production.

Will the chef know exactly what to make?
Yes. Every approved order generates a chef-ready job card with final design reference, cake size, flavour, theme, message, colours, material checklist, delivery slot, and special notes.

Will the owner understand costing?
Yes. The system shows estimated material cost, complexity, suggested selling price, payment status, and margin band. The owner can manually override the price where needed.

Why not keep using WhatsApp?
WhatsApp is good for discovery and quick conversation. It is weak as the final source of truth for custom production. The proposed system keeps WhatsApp as an entry channel, but moves final ordering, approval, costing, and job card creation into a structured platform.
